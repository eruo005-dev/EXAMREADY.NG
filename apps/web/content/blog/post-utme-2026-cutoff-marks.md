Most students hear "JAMB cut-off" and think that's the bar. It's not. The JAMB national cut-off (typically 140-160) is just the floor; **the university's own Post-UTME cut-off** is the real bar, and it varies hugely by university and course.

This guide covers the top 10 universities in Nigeria by admissions competitiveness, with the JAMB scores typically required for admission and the Post-UTME screening process for each.

> **Verify the latest cut-offs on each university's official admissions portal.** Cut-offs change year-to-year based on application volume — what's listed below is based on 2024-2025 patterns and the trend across recent years. Always confirm before relying on a number.

## What "cut-off mark" actually means

There are typically **three** cut-offs that matter:

1. **National cut-off** — JAMB's published floor, usually 140-160
2. **University cut-off** — what the university accepts as the JAMB minimum (e.g. UI usually requires 200+, UNILAG 220+)
3. **Course cut-off** — what your specific course asks for (e.g. Medicine at UI is typically 290+, even though UI's general cut-off is 200)

Then there's the **Post-UTME aggregate**, which combines:

- Your JAMB score (50%)
- Your Post-UTME screening score (50%)
- Sometimes your O'Level grades

So your Post-UTME bar isn't just "scored above the cut-off in JAMB" — it's "scored above the AGGREGATE cut-off after Post-UTME."

## Top 10 universities — typical cut-off ranges

Listed in approximate competitiveness order. **All numbers are typical ranges from recent years; verify on the official portal of each.**

### 1. University of Ibadan (UI)

- **University cut-off**: 200+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy**: 290-310 JAMB; aggregate above 70%
- **Law, Engineering**: 250-280 JAMB
- **Arts, social sciences**: 200-230 JAMB
- **Screening**: CBT-style, 50 questions, English + general knowledge

### 2. University of Lagos (UNILAG)

- **University cut-off**: 220+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy, Law**: 290-310
- **Engineering, Sciences**: 240-280
- **Screening**: now mostly via documentation review (no exam), with an aggregate score combining JAMB + O'Level

### 3. Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU)

- **University cut-off**: 200+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy, Engineering**: 270-290
- **Screening**: O'Level grade-based aggregate; no Post-UTME exam in most recent years

### 4. Ahmadu Bello University (ABU Zaria)

- **University cut-off**: 180+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy**: 260-280
- **Engineering**: 230-260
- **Screening**: documentation + O'Level aggregate

### 5. University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN)

- **University cut-off**: 200+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy**: 270-290
- **Engineering, Sciences**: 230-260
- **Screening**: CBT-style 50-question exam covering English + general knowledge

### 6. University of Benin (UNIBEN)

- **University cut-off**: 200+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy**: 270-290
- **Law, Engineering**: 230-270
- **Screening**: CBT exam

### 7. University of Port Harcourt (UNIPORT)

- **University cut-off**: 180+ JAMB
- **Medicine**: 260-280
- **Engineering**: 220-250
- **Screening**: documentation review + O'Level

### 8. Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA)

- **University cut-off**: 180+ JAMB
- **Engineering courses**: 240-260
- **Screening**: documentation + O'Level

### 9. University of Ilorin (UNILORIN)

- **University cut-off**: 200+ JAMB (notable for high consistency)
- **Medicine, Law**: 250-280
- **Engineering, Sciences**: 220-250
- **Screening**: now primarily documentation/aggregate-based

### 10. Lagos State University (LASU)

- **University cut-off**: 180+ JAMB
- **Medicine, Pharmacy**: 250-280
- **Law**: 220-250
- **Screening**: documentation + O'Level + JAMB

## What this means for your strategy

If you're targeting a top-10 federal university for a competitive course (Medicine, Pharmacy, Law, Engineering), the realistic JAMB range is **270-310**. Anything below 250 puts you at high risk of falling below the aggregate cut-off after Post-UTME.

If you're targeting a state university or a less competitive course, the bar drops to **200-240** — meaningful margin to absorb a slightly weaker subject.

**Match your university choice to your realistic JAMB score**, not to where your friends are going. Many students apply to UNILAG with a JAMB of 200, knowing nothing about UNILAG's typical 220+ floor for non-Medicine courses, and lose the year.

## How Post-UTME screening differs by university

Not all universities run a screening exam any more. Three patterns:

### CBT-style screening exam

Universities like UI, UNN, and UNIBEN run a 50-90 minute CBT exam covering English Language + general knowledge questions. Practice the format. The questions resemble JAMB English in structure but include current-affairs items.

### Documentation review only

UNILAG, OAU, and UNILORIN largely moved away from screening exams in recent years. They take an aggregate of JAMB + O'Level and rank candidates. **Strong O'Level grades matter as much as JAMB here** — A1 in 5+ subjects vs C-grades changes your ranking dramatically.

### Hybrid

A few universities (LASU, RSU) combine documentation with a short oral or written assessment. Verify with the specific university's admissions office.

## How to study for Post-UTME screening

If your university has a CBT exam, the best preparation is:

1. **Past Post-UTME papers** for that specific university — most universities don't publish them, but they're in circulation through past students. Ask your seniors.
2. **JAMB English at high difficulty** — the screening exam often pulls from a similar pattern but slightly harder.
3. **Current affairs** — Nigerian politics, economy, and recent international events. The screening exams typically include 10-15 current-affairs questions.
