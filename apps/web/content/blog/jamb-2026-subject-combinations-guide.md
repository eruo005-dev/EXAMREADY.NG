Picking the wrong JAMB subject combination is the single most expensive avoidable mistake in Nigerian university admissions. JAMB requires English Language plus three subjects relevant to your course. Pick the wrong three and your application is automatically ineligible — no appeal, no second chance, no university will admit you to that course.

This guide gives the JAMB subject combinations for the 30+ most common courses. **Verify on the JAMB CAPS portal before registering** — combinations can shift year-to-year, and individual universities sometimes add their own requirements on top.

## How to use this guide

1. Find your course in the table below
2. Note the JAMB subjects (English + 3 others)
3. Verify your O'Level subject combination matches the course's separate O'Level requirements (different topic — see your university's prospectus)
4. **Confirm on JAMB CAPS / your university's portal before submitting your JAMB application**

If your course isn't listed, check our [subject combinations tool](/tools/subject-combinations) which has the full database, or the JAMB brochure.

## Education

Most Education courses follow the subject pattern of what they teach plus English. So **Education English** = English + Literature + 2 arts; **Education Mathematics** = English + Maths + Physics + one science; etc. Specific combinations are listed by each university.

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Taking Biology for Engineering thinking it's "biology-flexible"** — it's not. Engineering needs Mathematics + Physics + Chemistry.
2. **Skipping Mathematics for Economics-related courses** — Accounting, Banking, Business Admin all require Maths even though students often think Economics alone is enough.
3. **Confusing CRK/IRK as a "free pass"** — they're accepted as flexible options, but at competitive courses they sometimes carry slightly less weight in the aggregate vs Government or History.
4. **Taking a foreign language thinking it counts** — it usually doesn't for most courses. Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa/French at JAMB level only counts for arts and language courses.
5. **Mismatching JAMB subjects with O'Level** — JAMB requires the subjects you take in JAMB; O'Level requirements are a SEPARATE list per course. Verify both.
